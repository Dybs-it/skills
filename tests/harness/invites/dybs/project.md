# Invites

A small library that issues and redeems invite links for team workspaces.

## Why
Teams need to let new members in without an admin adding each one by hand.

## Who
- **For:** team owners and the people they invite
- **Built by:** one developer with AI agents

## Now
- Create and redeem work, in memory. Tests: `npm test`.
