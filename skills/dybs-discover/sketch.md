# Stage 2: Sketch, explore solutions

Goal: several genuinely different ways to solve the target from the map,
concrete enough to compare, before anyone commits to one.

## Steps

1. **Research (lightning demos).** Find how others solve this or a similar
   problem: competitors, products from other fields, known patterns, open
   source. Use the market researcher. Each find is one line: what it is,
   the idea worth borrowing, the link. Two to five finds.
2. **Notes.** From the map and the research, list the ideas, constraints
   and opportunities worth keeping. Short; this is raw material.
3. **Concepts.** Two to four solutions that differ in kind, not in
   features: who does the work, how much is automated, where it happens,
   what it costs the user. When sub-agents are available, give each
   concept to its own, each with a different lens (the smallest thing that
   could work, the most automated, the most human, the boldest), so they
   do not converge on one idea.
4. **For each concept:** a name, a one-line pitch, the user's experience as
   5 to 8 steps through the target moment, its benefits, its limits and
   trade-offs, and the assumptions it depends on.

The human may bring their own concept at any point: take it as one of the
set, written up like the others. Combining concepts, or asking for another
round, is always allowed.

Do not rank the concepts here. Choosing is the next stage.

**Specialists:** the market researcher for step 1; counsel when a concept
handles money, personal data or a regulated activity.

## Readiness checklist

**core**
- [ ] At least two research finds, each with a link, or the reason there are none
- [ ] At least two concepts that differ in kind, not only in features
- [ ] Each concept's experience through the target moment, step by step
- [ ] Each concept's benefits, limits and assumptions

**thorough**
- [ ] Three or four concepts, including the smallest one that could work
- [ ] Research outside the obvious competitors (another field, an analogue process)
- [ ] For each concept, what it would take to build, roughly
- [ ] The human's own ideas included and written up like the others

## Done when

The human says they have enough different concepts to choose from.
