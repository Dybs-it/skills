# Stage 3: Decide, choose a direction

Goal: one direction the human chose, the reasons, what was left behind, and
a storyboard of the experience the prototype must show.

## Steps

1. **Review.** Show the concepts side by side: one line each for the
   experience, the benefit, the risk.
2. **Critique.** For each concept: strengths, weaknesses, feasibility, and
   which question from the map it answers. Bas (the skeptic) speaks on every
   concept. Critique the concepts, never the human.
3. **Decision.** Ask the human to choose a concept, or to combine named
   parts of several. Recommend one and say why. **Never choose for them.**
   With `decides: team` or `client`, the decision waits for the named
   decider.
4. **Rationale.** Record why it was chosen and why each alternative was
   rejected, in the human's words where possible.
5. **Storyboard.** The critical experience as 6 to 12 frames, from the
   moment the user meets the product to the moment they get the outcome.
   Each frame: what the user sees, what they do, what happens. Only what is
   needed to test the central assumption, not every feature.
6. **To validate.** The assumptions the prototype must test, the riskiest
   first, each with what result would change the decision.
7. **Prototype scope.** What the first build must contain to walk through
   the storyboard, and what it leaves out on purpose.

A weak decision is challenged once (see "Respect the decider" in
`SKILL.md`), then recorded.

**Specialists:** counsel when the chosen direction handles money, personal
data or a regulated activity and nobody has checked it.

## Readiness checklist

**core**
- [ ] The chosen direction, in the human's words, with the reason
- [ ] Every rejected concept, with why
- [ ] A storyboard of 6 to 12 frames through the target moment
- [ ] The assumptions the prototype must test, riskiest first
- [ ] The prototype scope: in, and left out on purpose

**thorough**
- [ ] What result in testing would change the decision
- [ ] Each question from the map: answered, or carried into the prototype
- [ ] Risks of the chosen direction, each with an owner
- [ ] Legal or compliance points checked, or marked not applicable with the reason

## Done when

The human approves the direction and the storyboard. Say plainly that a
direction is chosen and that nothing is validated yet. The prototype scope
is what `dybs-task` turns into the first tasks.
